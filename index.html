§ 02
★ Featured — M.S. Thesis

Periodicity-Based Triage of APT Command-and-Control Beaconing in Network Flows Using Machine Learning

University of Nevada, Las Vegas · 2025 – 2026  ·  View on UNLV Repository →

Malware beaconing to command-and-control infrastructure often creates periodic callback behavior in network flow logs, even when payloads are encrypted. This research builds a machine-learning pipeline that ranks suspicious communication units for analyst review using only flow timestamps, engineering nine periodicity-based features, including inter-arrival time regularity, FFT-based spectral concentration, autocorrelation, and sub-window stability, and training a supervised Random Forest ranker on CTU-13 with controlled synthetic beacon injection. The pipeline is validated across five CTU-13 scenarios covering different malware families and C2 styles.

Private & Secure AI · 2026
